The club

You shouldn't have to shrink to fit a gym. We built one around you.

Platinum began with a simple observation: most women's fitness journeys stall not from lack of will, but from rooms that never felt like theirs. So every studio we open is women-only — the floor, the classes, the trainers, the changing rooms.

Today, three studios across Hyderabad run on the same idea. A nineteen-year-old learning to lift, a mother rebuilding after delivery, a teacher in her forties with a thyroid diagnosis, a grandmother working on balance — all on the same floor, each with a plan of her own. Come with a goal, a diagnosis, a wedding date or a bad week. You'll get a trainer who listens, a programme that fits your life, and a room that lets you get on with it.

Clinically informed fitness

Training built around a diagnosis, not despite it.

Most gyms hand every woman the same plan. We start with your history — hormones, joints, blood work, medication, age — and build from there, with your doctor's guidance where it matters.

01 PCOS & PCOD Strength-first training for insulin resistance, with cardio dosed to support rather than exhaust.
02 Thyroid conditions Programmes that work with fatigue and slow metabolism — consistent, low-friction, sustainable.
03 Type 2 diabetes & pre-diabetes Resistance training and walking protocols that support blood-sugar management alongside medical care.
04 Hypertension & heart health Moderate-intensity conditioning with breathing and recovery built into every session.
05 Menopause & perimenopause Load-bearing work for bone density and muscle, plus habits for sleep, hot flushes and mood.
06 Post-partum recovery A staged return to training — breath, core, pelvic floor and posture first, load later.
07 Bone health Progressive resistance and balance work for osteopenia, osteoporosis and fall prevention.
08 Knee, back & joint pain Low-impact options, machine-led strength and mobility work that respects the joint you're protecting.
09 Metabolic health & obesity Slow, measurable fat loss without crash diets — built on strength, steps and food you'll actually eat.

We are trainers, not doctors. We do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. We train within the guidance you bring from your physician or physiotherapist, and we will ask for it before we load a joint or change a programme. If something doesn't feel right, we stop and refer you on.

Questions

Before you come in.

Still unsure? Call the studio nearest to you. A real person answers, Monday to Saturday.

Talk to a studio
Is Platinum Fitness Club really women-only?

Yes. All three Hyderabad studios — Malakpet, Attapur and Saidabad — are women-exclusive. The floor, classes and training are for women only, and our trainers are women.

Can I try the gym before joining?

Yes. Every studio offers a free trial session with a body assessment. Call your nearest branch or WhatsApp 98493 40257 to book a slot, Monday to Saturday.

What are the timings?

Malakpet and Attapur are open Monday to Saturday, 6:00 am to 9:00 pm. Saidabad is open Monday to Saturday, 6:15 am to 8:30 pm. All three studios are closed on Sundays.

Do you help with PCOS and thyroid weight loss?

Yes. Our trainers build strength, cardio and habit plans around PCOS and thyroid conditions, working alongside your doctor's guidance. The focus is sustainable fat loss — not crash dieting.

Is EMS training available?

Yes. EMS sessions are available at our Attapur studio: a 20-minute, low-impact full-body workout that is useful when your schedule is tight or your joints need a gentler start. Malakpet and Saidabad do not offer EMS.

Where are your women's gyms in Hyderabad?

Malakpet: 1st Floor, Zudio Building, opposite Metro Pillar 1456, Anand Nagar Colony, 500036. Attapur: Plot 396, 1st Floor, Shree Bhagya Laxmi Plaza, opposite Pillar 151, 500048. Saidabad: 1st Floor, above Ratnadeep Supermarket, Zahoor Bagh, Shankeshwar Bazar, 500059.

What does membership cost?

Plans run for 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, and the current offer gives three months plus one month free for new members. Message your studio on WhatsApp for the current plan sheet — inclusions and validity are confirmed in writing before you join.

Do you have sauna and steam?

Yes — sauna and steam are part of the club's recovery facilities, along with lockers and showers. Availability can differ slightly by studio, so confirm with your branch when you book.

Is the training suitable for all ages?

Yes. Our members range from women in their twenties to women in their sixties, and every programme is scaled to age, experience and medical history. Older members train with a trainer nearby, on machines and loads chosen for joints and balance rather than ego.

Is this clinical fitness or medical treatment?

It is fitness, informed by clinical guidance. Our trainers are fitness professionals, not doctors — we do not diagnose or treat conditions. For PCOS, thyroid, diabetes, hypertension, menopause, bone health or post-partum recovery, we build the training around the advice you bring from your physician or physiotherapist, and we refer you back when a question belongs to them.
