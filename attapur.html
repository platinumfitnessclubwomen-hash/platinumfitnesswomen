The studio

A women-only floor in the middle of Attapur.

Attapur is our highest-rated studio — 4.9 from 666 reviews — on the first floor of Shree Bhagya Laxmi Plaza, opposite Pillar 151.

Attapur questions

Before you visit.

Call the studio and a real person will answer — Monday to Saturday, during opening hours.

Call 098493 40257
What are the Attapur studio timings?

Monday to Saturday, 6:00 am to 9:00 pm. The studio is closed on Sundays.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — a free trial session with a body assessment. Call the studio or WhatsApp 98493 40257 to book a slot, Monday to Saturday.

How do I reach the studio?

We are on the first floor of Shree Bhagya Laxmi Plaza, opposite Pillar No. 151 in Attapur. Look for the Platinum board above the shops.

Do you have Pilates and sauna?

Yes. Pilates and core work are part of the class schedule, and sauna and steam are available as recovery facilities. Confirm current class timings with the studio.

Which areas do you serve?

Members travel to us from Attapur, Mehdipatnam, Tolichowki, Rajendranagar, Langar Houz, Gudimalkapur, Hyderguda and Bandlaguda.

Do you train women of all ages?

Yes. Members range from women in their twenties to women in their sixties. Programming is scaled to age, experience and medical history — older members train with a trainer nearby, on machines chosen for joints and balance.

Is the training clinically informed?

Yes — PCOS, thyroid, diabetes, hypertension, menopause, bone health and post-partum programmes are built around the guidance you bring from your doctor or physiotherapist. We are trainers, not doctors, and we do not diagnose or treat conditions.
