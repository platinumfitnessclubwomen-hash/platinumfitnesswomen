The branch

A women-only floor in the middle of Saidabad.

Our newest branch, above Ratnadeep Supermarket in Saidabad — 5.0 from 40 reviews, with new equipment and a calmer floor. Saidabad is a gym-only floor: no group classes, just equipment, space and trainers.

Saidabad opened most recently, which means the equipment is new, the floor is quiet, and the trainers have time for women who are just starting. It is smaller and calmer than Attapur — many members describe it as the easiest place to begin.

Being above Ratnadeep means errands and a workout fit into one trip. Come in the morning for a nearly empty floor, or in the early evening for a one-to-one session. There are no group classes on this floor — the equipment is yours when you need it.

Saidabad questions

Before you visit.

Call the branch and a real person will answer — Monday to Saturday, during opening hours.

Call 092810 22891
What are the Saidabad branch timings?

Monday to Saturday, 6:15 am to 8:30 pm. The branch is closed on Sundays.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — a free trial session with a body assessment. Call the branch or WhatsApp 98493 40257 to book a slot, Monday to Saturday.

How do I reach the branch?

We are on the first floor above Ratnadeep Supermarket at Zahoor Bagh, Shankeshwar Bazar, Saidabad. The entrance is from the Shankeshwar Bazar side.

Is this a good branch for beginners?

Yes. Saidabad is our newest and calmest floor, and personal training is available if you would like a trainer beside you for the first few weeks.

Do you run group classes at Saidabad?

No. Saidabad is a gym-only branch with no group classes on the floor. What you get is a quiet, well-equipped gym with female trainers, strength and cardio machines, personal training and one-to-one mobility work. If you want yoga, Pilates, Zumba or aerobics classes, our Malakpet and Attapur branches run those through the week.

Which areas do you serve?

Members travel to us from Saidabad, Saroornagar, Dilsukhnagar, Madannapet, Champapet, Kothapet, Malakpet and Asmangadh.

Do you train women of all ages?

Yes. Members range from women in their twenties to women in their sixties, and the branch is a particularly easy place for beginners. Every programme is scaled to age, experience and medical history.

Is the training clinically informed?

Yes — PCOS, thyroid, post-partum, menopause and joint-friendly programmes are built around the guidance you bring from your doctor or physiotherapist. We are trainers, not doctors, and we do not diagnose or treat conditions.

Is Saidabad a women-only gym?

Yes. The Saidabad floor is women-only, with female trainers and a private, comfortable setting above Ratnadeep Supermarket in Zahoor Bagh.

What equipment is on the Saidabad floor?

You will find strength machines, free weights, cardio equipment and space for mobility and one-to-one mobility work. Personal training is available if you want a trainer beside you.

Is EMS training available at Saidabad?

No. EMS training is offered at our Attapur branch only. Saidabad is a gym-only floor focused on strength, cardio and personal training.
